Everything you may be wondering about adventures from The Pond, subscriptions, gifts, and correspondence with Tad.

01

The Adventures

What might be waiting inside?

What arrives each month?+

A new story from Tad and life around The Pond, original artwork, stickers, and curious little extras discovered along the way. Every adventure is a little different.

Is every month's adventure the same?+

Not at all. Each correspondence tells a new story from The Pond, with artwork and discoveries inspired by Tad's latest adventure.

Will I know what's coming ahead of time?+

Not entirely. Part of Wandered & Found is the pleasure of opening the envelope without knowing every little thing Tad has tucked inside.

02

Subscriptions & Gifts

Sending the adventure.

How does the monthly subscription work?+

Your first payment is made when you subscribe. After that, your subscription renews monthly until you cancel.

Can I cancel anytime?+

Yes. You can cancel your monthly subscription at any time.

Can I send an adventure as a gift?+

Yes. You can send someone a single adventure, a special one-time Birthday Adventure, or keep the adventures coming with a monthly gift subscription.

How much does it cost?+

Pricing is being finalized and will be announced before subscriptions open.

03

Shipping & Delivery

From The Pond to the mailbox.

Where do you ship?+

Shipping destinations and rates are being finalized before subscriptions open.

Can a gift be mailed directly to the recipient?+

Yes. When gift ordering opens, you'll be able to provide the recipient's name and shipping address so their adventure can travel directly to their mailbox.

Correspondence with Tad

Can I write back to Tad?

Absolutely. Tad is always pleased to receive correspondence from curious friends.

You'll be able to write to him the old-fashioned way or send him an email. His correspondence details will be posted here before the first adventures are mailed.
